Warung Sari Rasa — Dapur Kilat: an original Indonesian warung cooking game. Orders land on the ticket board, you grab ingredients from the counter, cook them on the stove, assemble the plate and serve before the customer's patience runs out.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser and play with keyboard and mouse.

## How to play

Orders appear on the ticket board with a patience timer. Click an ingredient on the counter (or press 1–6) to put it on the plate; ayam, telur and mie must cook on the stove first (4 seconds — take them off before they burn). Press Space or click SAJIKAN to serve a finished plate. Serve fast for combos, survive the 150-second shift, and climb 3 levels.
